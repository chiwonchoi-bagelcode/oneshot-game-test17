# Splash Rescue — Store listing (English)

## App name

Splash Rescue

## Short description

Wash away cute goo with one thumb and rescue the animals trapped inside!

## Full description

Purple goo has covered the forest, the village and the lake. Grab your water gun and let's clean it up!

**Move to dodge, stop to spray**
Put your thumb anywhere and drag to move. Let go, and your water gun sprays the nearest goo on its own. The longer you hold your ground, the more pressure builds and the thicker your stream gets, but standing still also makes you an easy target. Dodge or stand and spray: it's your call every time.

**That washing feeling**
Hit goo wobbles, shrinks and bursts into spray with a pop. The ground turns clean wherever your stream passes, and when a room is clear, the leftover goo washes away in one big wave.

**Rescue trapped animals**
Puppies, bunnies, foxes, otters and more are stuck in goo cocoons. Wash a cocoon off and the animal hops out and follows you around. Every friend you save moves into your shelter and gives you a bonus, like more health, more coins or a stronger stream.

**Runs take a minute or two**
Clear 3 to 5 rooms to finish an area. Each time you level up, pick one of three skill cards and build the run your way. Even if you lose, you keep every animal and coin from that run.

**From a tiny squirt gun to a giant water cannon**
Spend coins on stream power, range, health, pressure and splash, and your water gun evolves through 7 stages. The upgrade screen shows your stream getting thicker the moment you buy.

**What's inside**
- 30 areas across Sunny Forest, Windmill Village and Sparkle Lake, with 3 bosses
- 9 kinds of goo, each with its own trick
- 16 animal friends (two of them visit through the stamp card) and 8 shelter decorations
- 14 skill cards to pick from during a run
- A Daily Challenge with new rules each day, 3 daily tasks and a 7-day stamp card
- The Weekend Splash Festival: coins from areas ×1.5 every Saturday and Sunday
- Lose the same area twice and your friends come to cheer you on with a boost for that run

**Play at your own pace**
No ads and no in-app purchases. No hearts or energy, so play as long as you like. Made for one hand in portrait. Available in English and Korean.
